# Marketing & Communication Strategy

> Superseded for the launch by [17-LAUNCH-PLAN.md](17-LAUNCH-PLAN.md) (positioning, calendar, assets) and [18-FEASIBILITY.md](18-FEASIBILITY.md). Rule: no component prices in public copy.

> Goal: make people understand, in seconds, why an OpenForge agent is more than a picture, and sell 924 agents to people who will use them. Rule for every sentence: **show what the agent can do, never what it might earn.** No returns, yield, "passive income", floor or price talk ([01-ANALYSIS.md](01-ANALYSIS.md) §3.1).

## 1. Positioning

**Mascot and through-line:** **AX-1**, our CC0 robot with a phone for a brain ([14-AX1-BRAND.md](14-AX1-BRAND.md): character, voice, images).

**Tagline:** *A phone for a brain. Open for everything else.* · **Site headline:** *Build a robot. Your phone is the brain.*

**Three pillars** (every piece of content uses at least one):
1. **Understand it.** The Workbench takes 11 robots apart in interactive 3D; every part links to its open-source origin.
2. **Test it.** Every robot has missions with levels in the browser; SO-101 runs are replayed by a verifier, not self-reported.
3. **Build it.** AX-1 is CC0: Rover, Desk and Walker (community build), with every part and its open source listed. Each body carries a status: design / built / filmed.

**Why an NFT at all:** the lab is free; the 1,024 agents are its membership and record layer (wallet, on-chain record, Manual mode by default). Say it plainly, and say "skip the NFT if you only want the robot".

**Credibility assets:** CC0 everything we make, an open repo, fair on-chain reveal, safety by design (Manual mode, sale lock, firmware that holds instead of dropping), honest status labels (TWIN vs BUILD), and the automated test suite.

## 3. Messages by audience

- **Collectors:** "1,024 agents, the members of an open robotics lab. Each one owns a wallet and builds a record, failures included. Traits drawn on-chain after the sale: nobody, not even us, knows yours in advance."
- **Makers:** "Meet AX-1: a phone for a brain, open parts for the body. Test it in 3D first, then build it. All CC0."
- **Builders:** "Pilot the twin, replay it on your SO-101. Guided, in the browser, no code. Everything CC0."
- **On-chain natives:** "Every agent is an ERC-6551 account with a policy the holder controls: Manual, Assisted, Autonomous, a daily cap, an emergency pause and a sale lock. The operator can never move assets or sign."
- **Everyone:** "Play before you mint: the Proving Grounds is open to guests."

Words to use: lab, AX-1, twin, build, open, notebook, mission, record, verified, CC0.
Words to avoid: invest, earn, yield, returns, floor, moon, passive, guaranteed, profit.

## 4. The big idea: "Learn it. Test it. Build it."

AX-1 carries the story, in three acts:
1. **Act 1, Meet AX-1** (pre-launch): the face that looks at you, the phone-brain idea, the open build log. "AX-1 learns…" clips of the twin, failures included.
2. **Act 2, Prove it** (allowlist phase): everyone plays missions as a guest and enters the pilot ranking. Weekly "Pilot of the week", the first physical Rover build log, "Name AX-1's first trick".
3. **Act 3, Embody it** (mint and after): reveal stream, holders' first verified runs on-chain, the first 10 community builds on the builders' wall.

**Hero video (22 s, honest):** "A robot needs a brain." (illustration) → "You already own one." (mock-up or TWIN) → the AX-1 twin fails a mission, logs it, tries again → "Tested in 3D. Experimental in hardware." → AX-1's face, tagline, "Play the mission". Every shot tagged ILLUSTRATION, TWIN or BUILD. When the first real AX-1 runs, swap in a twin-vs-real side by side; not earlier. Storyboard in [14-AX1-BRAND.md](14-AX1-BRAND.md).
- **Capture is built in:** "Record clip" in the Proving Grounds and Film mode in Robot Link produce ready-to-post videos.

## 7. Content pillars (with examples)

1. **Build log** (35%): AX-1 parts, wiring, first moves; "what a phone gives a robot for free".
2. **Learn** (25%): Workbench and Codex clips, one robot or one part per post.
3. **Proof** (25%): missions, leaderboards, verified runs, fair-reveal transparency.
4. **Agents & trust** (15%): wallet explainers, "lock before you list", "we never DM first".

Formats: 15–30 s vertical clips (AX-1 face, Workbench stages, twin runs), threads with GIFs, livestreams for builds, reveal and AMAs.

## 8. Partnerships and outreach

- **Robotics:** LeRobot / SO-101 builders and makers who sell or build the arm. Offer: free Robot Link onboarding, a Prime-style showcase of their replays, allowlist spots for real setups.
- **ERC-6551 / agent ecosystem:** wallets, agent frameworks and token-bound account tooling. Offer: case study of the policy model (L1/L2/L3, sale lock).
- **CC0 community:** CC0 artists and collectives. Offer: remix contests, with credit in the gallery.
- **Creators:** 10–20 mid-size creators in NFT art, robotics and on-chain dev. Paid placements must be labelled as ads; no price or return claims, ever.

## 9. Community management

- Discord roles: Visitor → Pilot (has a verified run) → Holder (verified wallet) → Builder (shows a robot or CC0 work).
- Weekly rituals: Pilot of the week (Mon), build showcase (Wed), Forge Report or AMA (Fri).
- Moderation: no price talk, no DMs from "team", report button pinned. Two moderators in different time zones before the allowlist opens.

**How we measure:**
- `npm run kpi -- mainnet` prints every on-chain and verifier KPI above and saves a dated JSON:
  - minted and holders;
  - wallets with a mode set or locked;
  - agents with a record;
  - attested runs;
  - pilot trials and eligible pilots.
- Site traffic comes from Cloudflare Web Analytics (cookie-less, no banner).
- Run the report weekly and paste it in the Forge Report.

## 11. Budget (lean)

Proceeds are at most about 11 ETH net, and the audit comes first. Marketing spend before mint stays small:
- creator placements (only after the hero video exists);
- moderators;
- a few SO-101 kits given to builders, which become content and proof;
- the reveal stream production.

Everything else is organic and built from the product itself (leaderboard, clips, CC0 remixes).

## 12. Risks and how we talk about them

| Risk | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "AI NFT" fatigue and skepticism | Show, don't claim: live links to the Proving Grounds, the repo, the virtual arm. |
| Securities / MiCA perception | Never mention returns. Agents are collectibles with tools; the docs say so. |
| Scams and fake mints | One official link, pinned everywhere. Never DM first. Mint only on OpenSea. |
| Reveal fairness questioned | Publish the commit block, the reveal block, the seed and the generator hash; anyone can regenerate the art. |
| Real-arm claims ahead of reality | Say "supported" until the team's first physical replay is filmed; then show it. |
| Slow sale | The Safe can extend the deadline (up to +90 days); communicate it early and calmly. |

**Series: "Which robot is your agent?"** One class, its real open platform, its Workbench page and mission ([docs/research/open-robot-catalog.md](research/open-robot-catalog.md)). End each post with "Which one would you build first?". Never copy third-party designs into our CC0 assets, and never re-host non-commercial (NC) files: link to their repositories.
