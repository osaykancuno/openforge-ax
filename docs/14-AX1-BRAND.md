# 14 · AX-1 brand book

> Superseded for the launch by [17-LAUNCH-PLAN.md](17-LAUNCH-PLAN.md) (positioning, calendar, assets) and [18-FEASIBILITY.md](18-FEASIBILITY.md). Rule: no component prices in public copy.

AX-1 is the mascot, the logo and the flagship build of OpenForge AX: a small, original, CC0 robot whose head and brain is a phone. The brand is **OpenForge AX**, the robot is **AX-1**. Never call it "PhoneBot": that is UCLA's research project, credited as the inspiration for the architecture.

Assets: `npm run brand` renders everything in `art/brand/` (sources in `art/brand/src/`). Visual system: `docs/12-OPENFORGE-LAB.md` §4.

## 1. Character

AX-1 is a lab apprentice: a student of its own body.

| Trait | How it shows |
|---|---|
| **Curious** | Asks before it asserts; looks at whatever you show it. |
| **Candid** | Reports failures plainly: "fell over, 3rd try, here is why". Never spins. |
| **Patient, a little dry** | Small, understated humour. Not a hype man. |

**Voice:** short sentences, first person, present tense. Name the real part (IMU, servo, BLE). Use notebook numbers ("attempt 7", "112 g"), never prices. End on an observation or a question, not a pitch.

**Never:** price, floor, returns, "invest", "early", "don't miss"; crypto slang (gm, wagmi, LFG); cyberpunk or neon words; claims of feelings it can't back up (it "notices", "tries", "learns this session"); "it walks / drives / works" for a body not yet filmed on real hardware (say "the twin does", "the design aims to"); "better than" any named product.

**Catchphrases:** "Let me try that again." · "Fig. 01: I fell over. Noted." · "Show me the part." · "Open means you can check." · "Not yet. Here is what's missing."

**Face and poses.** Charcoal screen, two warm cream eyes, one short terracotta mouth line (`#c8553d` in 3D, `#b4472f` in print). Eyes track the viewer or the object.
- curious: head tilt, eyes wide;
- focused: eyes narrowed, mouth flat;
- failed: eyes down, body tilted;
- proud: a tiny upturn of the mouth, never a big smile.

Never: neon glow, red eyes, chrome, glitch, human skin, a real phone brand, logo or notch. Label renders **TWIN** and real photos **BUILD**.

## 2. Positioning

- **Tagline:** *A phone for a brain. Open for everything else.*
- **Site headline:** *Build a robot. Your phone is the brain.*
- **Pitch (25 words):** OpenForge Lab teaches how robots work in interactive 3D, lets you test them in browser missions, then helps you build AX-1, an open phone-brained robot, cheaply.

| Pillar | Proof |
|---|---|
| **Understand it** | The Workbench and the Codex are live; every part links to its open-source origin. |
| **Test it** | Missions in the browser for every robot; SO-101 runs are replayed by a verifier, not self-reported. |
| **Build it** | AX-1 is CC0: Rover, Desk and Walker (community build), every part listed with its open source. Each body carries a status label: design / built / filmed. |

**Standing honesty lines** (on every launch surface):
- "The AX-1 firmware is experimental and untested on hardware until our first build."
- "iPhone Safari has no Web Bluetooth: iPhone users drive the 3D twin."

**Why an NFT at all?**
> The lab is free. You don't need an NFT to learn, play missions or build AX-1. The collection is the lab's membership and record layer: 1,024 agents, each with its own wallet and an on-chain record of what it verifiably did. It is CC0, so anyone can remix the art. It is a collectible with tools, not an investment, and we make no promise about value. If you only want the robot, skip the NFT.

## 4. Community mechanics (never financial)

1. **Name AX-1's first trick.** Replies open 7 days, the team shortlists 5, Discord vote (accounts older than 30 days). The prize is credit in the Codex.
2. **Build-along.** Weekly Rover build log; community photos tagged #AX1Build need a handwritten note or a timestamped build page. AI "builds" are removed.
3. **First 10 builders wall.** The first 10 people to document a working AX-1 body running one mission on video. The team reviews each entry; there is no payment or allowlist reward, so there is nothing to farm.
4. **Mission leaderboards.** Server-replayed runs only, daily caps, public rules, an appeal path. Ranks are never tied to money.
5. **Codex contributors.** Original or compatibly licensed explanations, reviewed by two maintainers, credited in the entry.

No token or ETH rewards; no giveaways that require holding or buying; no DMs from the team, ever.
