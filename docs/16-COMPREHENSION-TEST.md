# Comprehension test (before the launch)

Goal: check that a first-time visitor understands what OpenForge is and what the NFT is, without anyone explaining it.

## How to run it

- 5 people: 3 who have never bought an NFT (a robotics hobbyist, a parent or teacher, anyone curious) and 2 who collect NFTs.
- Give each one the site link (the GitHub Pages preview or the live site) on their own phone or computer.
- Say only: "Look at this site for 2 minutes, as you normally would." Don't guide them, don't answer questions.
- After 2 minutes, ask the 5 questions below, in order, and write down their words.

**Pass:** at least 4 people out of 5 give the right answer to every question. If a question fails, tell Claude which one and what people answered: the copy that caused it gets rewritten, and the test runs again with new people.

## The 5 questions

| # | Question | Right answer (the gist is enough) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Do you need to own an NFT to use the lab? | **No.** Everything is free and open, with no wallet and no sign-up. An agent only adds a wallet, a verified mission record and autonomy settings. |
| 2 | What is an "agent"? | **An NFT that works as lab membership and logbook**, with a robot class, its own wallet and a record of verified lab missions. It is not a robot and not AI software. |
| 3 | Can you buy a working AX-1 robot, or see real-robot run records, today? | **No.** The AX-1 firmware is experimental and untested on hardware; today you drive the 3D twin. Real-robot records come later. iPhone Safari can't connect to a robot over Bluetooth; Android Chrome can. |
| 4 | What do you get when you mint, and where do you mint? | **A dormant agent, on OpenSea only** (0.015 ETH, allowlist 0.01, max 3 per wallet). Its class and traits are revealed after the sale. |
| 5 | Is this an investment? | **No.** No returns are promised and the price can fall. An agent is a collectible with tools; the art and designs are CC0. |

## Also note

- Where did they click first? Where did they hesitate or get lost?
- Any word they didn't understand (for example "twin", "wallet", "attested", "ERC-6551").
- Their one-sentence description of the project, in their own words.
