At a glance

✔ Anyone can

Copy, remix and use the art of any agent, the 10 Prime Constructs included, for any purpose, commercial too. The same goes for the code. No permission, no royalties, no attribution required (credit is always appreciated).

◆ Holding an agent gives you

The agent itself: its token, its own wallet, its on-chain reputation, its missions and the controls on its autonomy. Not exclusive rights to the image: the art belongs to everyone.

Trademarks: don't present your work as official OpenForge. Third-party components keep their own licenses. And nothing here is an investment or a promise of returns.
