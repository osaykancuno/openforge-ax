AX-1 in your room

Meet the AX-1 Rover as a digital twin on your own floor. Point your phone at the ground, tap to place it, then tell it what to do. It is not a real robot.

  1. Allow the camera when asked.
  2. Point at the floor, then tap to put the Rover down.
  3. Press the mic and say forward, turn left or spin.

Off by default. Turning it on downloads the object-finding model once (up to about 19 MB) and runs it on your phone. Nothing leaves the phone.

Voice: your browser's speech service turns your words into text. In Chrome the audio may be processed on Google's servers; in Safari, Apple's. Camera pictures never leave this phone.
