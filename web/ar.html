My IRL lab

Meet the AX-1 Rover as a digital twin on your own floor. Point your phone at the ground, tap to place it, then drive it with the buttons. It is not a real robot. Camera pictures never leave this phone.

Choose a body
  1. Allow the camera when asked.
  2. Point at the floor, then tap to put the Rover down.
  3. Tap a button for a short move, or hold Forward, Back, Left or Right to keep moving. STOP always stops it.

Off by default. Turning it on downloads the object-finding model once (up to about 19 MB) and runs it on your phone. Nothing leaves the phone.

For "Take a photo" in AR: lets this page read the camera picture when you take one. It stays on the phone unless you share it.

Apple AR shows the twin at its real size on your floor: walk around it. To drive it, use the camera view.
