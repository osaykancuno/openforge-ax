Trouble connecting?
  • Use Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer (phones and Firefox/Safari cannot talk to USB devices).
  • Power the arm with its own supply (5–7.4 V or 12 V, matching your servos) and plug in the USB cable.
  • Waveshare bus board: the jumper must be on B (USB).
  • No port in the list? Click "Show all ports". On Windows install the board's USB driver (usually "CH343" from WCH). On Linux add yourself to the dialout group and remove brltty.
  • Only one servo found, as ID 1? New servos all ship as ID 1: set the IDs once with LeRobot's motor setup, or ask your kit seller.
  • Comfortable with Python? scripts/real/replay_so101.py does the same through LeRobot.
